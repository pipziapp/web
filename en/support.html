Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Open the Pro screen in Pipzi and tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pipzi. Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Pipzi. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple or Google, so refunds are requested from them: reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or the order in your Google Play account.

Where is my progress stored?

On your device only — Pipzi has no accounts. Deleting the app deletes your progress and imported books.
